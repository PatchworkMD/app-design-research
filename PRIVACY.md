# PatchworkMD plugin privacy policy

Effective: 2026-09-30. Applies to Hugging App 0.1.6, Dreamer 1.3.0, Unicycle 0.1.1, and earlier public versions where applicable. PatchworkMD is the publisher's public brand and is not a separate registered entity.

## Hugging App

Hugging App asks your AI host to review screenshots, interface text, or code that you supply. The material can contain personal or confidential information. The package does not upload it to a PatchworkMD server. Website research is disabled by the public skill.

## Dreamer

Dreamer reads receipts, files, and feedback that you select. Its local helpers make no network calls. The preference sync helper reads only the target configuration, proposal, and Markdown files that you specify. After you approve an exact preview, it writes the approved text to those targets and saves backups and transaction records in your configured local directory. It does not scan folders or run in the background.

Dreamer skills may send the material you provide to your AI host for analysis. The host may process it remotely under its own settings and policies.

## Unicycle

Unicycle coordinates Codex tasks through the host's native task tools. Its local helper stores task cursors and delivery receipts in the state directory you select. It does not read internal Codex databases, send messages on its own, or run a separate speech service. The Codex host controls task access, message delivery, and speech processing.

## Host processing and local files

Your AI application, its model provider, and any organization that administers it may process inputs and outputs under their own settings and policies. A local helper does not make cloud inference local. These packages do not promise zero retention, a particular processing region, or a provider's training policy.

Dreamer backups and Unicycle cache files stay in the local directories configured for those tools. They remain there until you remove them. Uninstalling a plugin does not delete files that its local helper created or records retained by your AI host.

PatchworkMD does not receive review material, receipts, preferences, task details, or local cache contents through these packages. If you contact support or open a public issue, the service you use and its provider process your message. Do not send credentials, confidential files, or private screenshots through public support channels.

## Support and changes

For support or privacy requests, contact [PatchworkMD on X](https://x.com/patchworkmd). This is not a confidential upload channel. For records held by your AI host or social platform, use that provider's privacy controls and policy.

Changes to this policy will be recorded with the relevant release.
